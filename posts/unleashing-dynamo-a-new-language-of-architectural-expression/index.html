Unleashing Dynamo: A New Language of Architectural Expression

October 8, 2023 Andres Silva

The challenge was clear: translate intricate mathematical calculations into tangible design. The tool to do it wasn’t obvious — until Dynamo appeared.

The Discovery

Initially unaware of the coding potential embedded within Revit, the discovery of Dynamo was both surprising and transformative. Hesitation came first — yet that hesitation proved completely unwarranted. Dynamo turned out to be a game-changer: a tool of boundless versatility and practicality that changed the fundamental approach to design.

Dynamo’s visual coding paradigm offers a canvas where code elements interconnect, each arrangement of nodes yielding a different spectrum of outcomes. Like Grasshopper in Rhino or Catia Design’s broadcasting functionality, it allows the creation of custom definitions — a language of design that echoes and realizes what was previously only conceptual.

From Tutorial to Triumph

The early stages involved immersing in tutorials, hopeful but cautious. The breakthrough came when code snippets from various sources, combined in new permutations, began aligning with the vision for the roof structure. Through a process of exploration and script dissection, the code took shape — leveraging references to pinpoint support placement, dictate spacing, and determine section type, thickness, and structural purpose for each element.

What the Script Actually Produces

While the script may appear unrefined at first inspection, its power lies in its ability to breathe life into the structure — generating a structural system that perfectly mirrors the complex geometry established in Part I of the rail station project. The renders that follow bear witness to the script’s profound capacity to yield structural complexity and spatial richness from an apparently simple set of coded instructions.

This is Dynamo’s defining contribution to architectural practice: the gap between mathematical intent and built form, which once required years of specialized software expertise, collapses into a set of connected nodes. Mathematics becomes architecture, directly, without translation errors.
