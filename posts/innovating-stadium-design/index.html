Innovating Stadium Design: A Football Fanatic's Dream

October 8, 2023 Andres Silva

Introduction

Born out of a passion for football, this stadium in Brussels is a testament to architectural prowess and unwavering dedication. Months of meticulous development were invested to ensure the project embodied both functionality and aesthetic appeal. The structure itself is a paragon of sustainability, utilizing concrete infused with industrial waste for added resilience and panels woven from recycled fibers.

The Journey of Creation

The stadium’s initial form took shape through a process of sculpting layers around the z-axis — a crucial step in the development journey. Columns of precise thickness were strategically positioned to support the risers, meticulously aligning with the underlying beams through a well-crafted design algorithm.

Substructure, Panels, and Roof

The creation of a substructure to secure the panels, offset from the original concrete structure, was a pivotal task. Employing a Voronoi function ensured a flawless division that adhered to the design’s specified pattern. With the substructure in place, risers were integrated along with an alternating roof structure, interconnected seamlessly with the facade seams.

Seating and Facade Dynamics

The challenge of placing individual chairs was met with ingenuity, leveraging parameters for lines within Dynamo. The result was an array of seating that met the project’s initial constraints. Facade panels, engineered to open in a singular direction, demonstrated remarkable flexibility due to the building’s underlying geometry.

A Turning Point

An unforeseen challenge emerged when all chairs initially faced in one direction. A eureka moment came when a Dynamo code was devised, expertly rotating the chairs towards the center of the stadium. The culmination of these efforts led to stunning renders, showcasing the seamless synergy between geometry and BIM. The inclusion of a tennis court underscored the stadium’s adaptability — its modular flooring poised to host an array of sports and entertainment events.
