Innovative Horizons: Unveiling My Design Odyssey

October 8, 2023 Andres Silva

Every architect has a defining moment — the instant when the built environment stops being background noise and becomes a living question demanding answers. For me, that moment came in Bogotá.

Roots of a Design Philosophy

Growing up in one of Latin America’s most architecturally layered cities, I was constantly surrounded by the tension between history and innovation. Colonial plazas sat beside glass towers. Informal settlements climbed hills adjacent to formal urban grids. This juxtaposition taught me early that design is never neutral — it is always a conversation between past constraints and future possibilities.

That philosophical foundation drives everything we do at Verdi Studio.

The Computational Turn

When I discovered parametric design tools — first Revit, then Dynamo — something fundamental shifted. Suddenly the gap between concept and execution narrowed dramatically. Complex geometries that would have taken weeks to manually coordinate could be generated, evaluated, and refined in hours.

But more than speed, what excited me was the intelligence these tools brought to design. Rather than iterating manually toward a good solution, we could enumerate entire solution spaces and let performance criteria select the best candidates.

What Verdi Studio Stands For

At Verdi Studio, we push boundaries at the intersection of parametric design, BIM, and sustainable performance. Our projects aren’t just visually compelling — they’re analytically grounded. Every facade panel, structural node, and floor plate is the result of deliberate computational decisions.

This blog is a window into that process: deep dives into generative design workflows, structural innovation, project management insights, and reflections on what it means to practice architecture in a data-driven age.

Welcome to the journey.
